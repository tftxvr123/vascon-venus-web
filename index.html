Timings, Guidelines & Resident Slot Reservation

Operational Timings & Batches

6:00 AM – 10:30 AM (All Residents)

Evening Session:

4:00 PM – 9:00 PM (All Residents)

⚠️ Closed on Tuesdays from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM for deep filtration and chlorination.

Dress Code & Mandatory Rules

  • Mandatory Swimwear: Proper nylon or spandex swimming costumes are compulsory. Cotton t-shirts/shorts are strictly prohibited.
  • Pre-Shower: Mandatory shower at the poolside shower stations before entering the water.
  • Kids Supervision: Children under 12 years must always be accompanied by an adult guardian.
  • Footwear & Food: Outside footwear and glassware/eatables are strictly not allowed on the pool deck.
